A game is its base GRP, `DUKE3D.GRP` or any `duke3d*.grp`, of Duke Nukem 3D 1.3D
or the Atomic Edition 1.4 or 1.5. Drop the GRP by itself anywhere on the Games
page and it gets a folder of its own, named after the file. Or drop a folder
holding it, named as you want the game called (`atomic`), and it is uploaded as
it is.

- Only the GRP is needed, and `DUKE.RTS` if your copy has one (the voices of
  Remote Ridicule): drop it after the GRP and choose the game. Leave the rest
  behind, DOSBox, manuals and launchers, which the game never reads.
- A DOS installation or CD has the GRP in `C:\DUKE3D`; a GOG or Steam copy in
  the game's folder, the Steam Megaton Edition in `gameroot/`. GOG's offline
  installer unpacks with `innoextract -e setup_duke3d_<version>.exe`.
- The 20th Anniversary World Tour, add-ons that need more than Duke's own CON
  files, and multiplayer do not play here.

#### Music

The music is in the GRP, played as on a Sound Blaster 16: nothing to add.

#### User maps

Drop a `.map` made with BUILD for Duke Nukem 3D, and choose the game when there
is more than one. It goes beside the GRP, and **Play on a map** offers it. The
shareware episode plays no user maps.
