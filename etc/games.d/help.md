Drop the GRP, or a folder holding it, named as you want the game called.

#### Duke Nukem 3D 1.3D or Atomic Edition 1.4/1.5

```text
atomic/
├── DUKE3D.GRP            the game, music and all
├── DUKE.RTS              optional, Remote Ridicule's voices
└── mymap.map             optional, a user map
```

#### Where your copy keeps it

- DOS or CD: `C:\DUKE3D\DUKE3D.GRP`
- GOG or Steam: the game's folder; Megaton Edition: `gameroot/`
- GOG's installer: `innoextract -e setup_duke3d_<version>.exe`

The 20th Anniversary World Tour is not known to play here.
